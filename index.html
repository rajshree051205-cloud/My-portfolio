Features actionable "How You Can Contribute" cards paired with a photo/video proof-of-action upload flow, plus a personalized, shareable recognition card with one-click sharing to LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram, and Facebook. Built solo and framework-free with a fully custom dark/light eco-themed animated UI; backend integration for live climate data and dynamic recognition cards is the next milestone.
